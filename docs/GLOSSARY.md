# Glossary

**Semantic IR**  
A structured intermediate representation intended to encode meaning rather than natural-language surface form.

**Semantic atom**  
A reusable discrete concept identifier that can participate in composition.

**Structural operator**  
A stable protocol primitive representing functions such as assertion, negation, causality, binding, querying, or constraints.

**Literal escape**  
A mechanism for transmitting exact surface data without semantic reinterpretation.

**Emergent communication**  
Communication protocols learned by interacting agents rather than fully specified by humans.

**Emergent language**  
A learned communication system with discrete or continuous signals that may develop vocabulary, syntax, grounding, or compositional structure.

**Neuralese**  
Informal/research term for internal or learned machine representations used for communication instead of human natural language.

**Latent communication**  
Direct transfer of neural representations such as embeddings, hidden states, or KV-cache-derived state between models/agents.

**Semantic communication**  
Communication optimized for preserving meaning, intent, or task utility rather than exact source symbols.

**Session dictionary**  
Temporary bindings that assign short references to recurring entities or compound concepts within one interaction.

**Compositionality**  
Ability to derive the meaning of a novel structure from the meanings of its components and their combination.

**Cross-model interoperability**  
Ability of independently trained or architecturally different models to exchange protocol messages consistently.

**Wire format**  
Concrete serialized bytes/tokens transmitted between endpoints.

**Debug syntax**  
Human-readable canonical representation of protocol semantics used for inspection and testing; distinct from the optimized wire format.
